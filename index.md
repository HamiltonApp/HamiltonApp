# Hamilton Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2026

Hamilton ("the app") is a read-only health data dashboard for Android.

## What Data Hamilton Accesses

Hamilton reads health and fitness data from Android Health Connect on your device. This covers the data types you explicitly grant it permission to read, which may include steps, distance, calories, weight, body fat, lean mass, bone mass, body water mass, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, blood pressure, respiratory rate, body temperature, skin temperature, sleep, workouts, nutrition, hydration and similar fitness and wellbeing metrics.

Hamilton reads only the data types you have granted, and accesses them solely to display them to you within the app. You choose which permissions to grant, and you can change or withdraw them at any time in Health Connect.

## What Hamilton Does With Your Data

Nothing. Hamilton displays your health metrics on screen and caches them locally on your device for performance. That is the full extent of what Hamilton does with your data.

Hamilton does not:

- Transmit any data to any server, cloud service, or third party
- Include any internet permission in the Android manifest (it cannot connect to the internet)
- Collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry
- Create user accounts or require sign-in
- Use advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks
- Write to or modify your Health Connect data (read-only access)

## Data Storage

Health data is cached in a local database on your device to reduce the number of Health Connect queries needed. This cache is stored in the app's private storage, which no other app can read, and it never leaves your device.

## Data Retention

**Hamilton's developer does not receive, store or retain any of your data.** Hamilton has no servers, no backend and no internet permission, so no copy of your information exists anywhere outside your own phone, and no developer-side retention period applies.

On your device, Hamilton's local cache is retained only for as long as you keep the app installed and its Health Connect permissions granted. Specifically:

- Cached health data is kept until you delete it using any of the methods in "Data Deletion" below.
- If you withdraw Hamilton's permission to read a particular type of health data in Health Connect, Hamilton deletes its cached copy of that data type the next time the app runs.
- If you withdraw all of Hamilton's Health Connect permissions, Hamilton deletes its entire local cache.
- Uninstalling Hamilton removes all of its stored data from your device.

Hamilton keeps only a rolling local copy of data that already exists in Health Connect on your device. It is a cache, not an archive — deleting it never affects the original data held by Health Connect or by the apps that recorded it.

## Data Deletion

**There is no data deletion request process, because there is no data to request the deletion of.** Hamilton never transmits your information anywhere, so the developer holds nothing about you and cannot delete anything on your behalf. You have complete and direct control over everything Hamilton stores, and you can delete it at any time using any of the following methods:

1. **Delete everything from inside Hamilton.** Open Hamilton, go to Settings and tap "Disconnect from Health Connect". Hamilton opens Health Connect so you can remove its access; once you do, Hamilton deletes its local copy of your data.
2. **Delete data for one metric.** In the Health Connect app, withdraw Hamilton's permission for any individual data type. Hamilton deletes its cached copy of that data type automatically.
3. **Clear all app storage.** On your phone, go to Settings → Apps → Hamilton → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes the entire local cache and all app settings immediately.
4. **Uninstall Hamilton.** Uninstalling removes all data Hamilton has stored on your device.

None of these actions delete the original records held in Health Connect or in the apps that wrote them — to remove those, use the Health Connect app directly.

If you have any question about data deletion, contact HamiltonApp@protonmail.com.

## Data Sharing

Hamilton shares no data with any third party. Hamilton has no mechanism to share data — it has no internet permission and no export functionality that sends data off-device.

## Your Controls

You can revoke Hamilton's Health Connect permissions at any time through Android Settings or the Health Connect app. You can also toggle individual metrics on or off within Hamilton's settings. To delete data Hamilton has stored, see "Data Deletion" above.

## Children's Privacy

Hamilton does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app has no account system and no way to identify any user's age.
